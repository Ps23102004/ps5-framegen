## Findings

**Blocker — §3.1 and §5.2. The proposed pacer does not guarantee the latency budget.** Four slots on a fixed 120 Hz grid are **33.333 ms**, and the plan’s **approximately 1 ms** median margin is arithmetically reasonable under its chosen overhead allowance. The problem is that §3 budgets the first eligible slot at or after the target, while §5.2 permits assignment *before* that target. The controller can spend more than the entire median margin by advancing a presentation. The plan should not claim the frame-generation target is reachable on this schedule until it defines and tests the same slot rule in both modes. [PLAN.md §§3.1, 5.2](~/Developer/ps5-framegen/PLAN.md:35)

Acceptance thresholds and proposed test inputs are **E** unless identified as the user’s target. Section, task, source-line and API identifiers are references.

### Blocker — §5.2. The slip rate is correct only for uninterrupted, ideal steady state

**Evidence.** On the nominal fast grid, **E** `120/(60000/1001) = 2.002`. Advancing by two slots loses **E 0.002 slot per output**. A bounded controller therefore needs one *net* insertion per **E 500 intervals = 8.342 s**, producing a **25 ms** hold. Over **E 600 s**, that is about **72** corrections before processing misses. On an exactly matched grid the steady-state beat is zero. At **E 50 ppm relative offset**, the beat is about `1/(59.94 × 0.000050) = 334 s`. The live clock offset is unmeasured. These calculations establish the required *net* corrections and say nothing about additional corrections in opposite directions. [PLAN.md §5.2](~/Developer/ps5-framegen/PLAN.md:118) · [Independent plan §5](~/Developer/ps5-framegen/reviews/01-astra-independent-plan.md:79)

| Case | Counterexample or consequence, all E |
|---|---|
| First second | The first ceiling gives `e₀` anywhere in `[0,S)`. With `e₀ = 0.8S`, the first eight observations remain above half a slot. The pacer removes a slot after about **0.12–0.13 s**, even with exactly matched clocks. At fast refresh that makes a **one-slot, 8.33 ms** interval. At matched refresh `+1−1 = 0`: two items target the same slot. With `e₀ = 0` on the fast grid, the first insertion comes around **4.3 s**, then roughly every **8.34 s**. |
| Jitter and deadline | On matched clocks, eight errors at `+0.51S` trigger removal. A subsequent block with uncorrected error `+0.49S` now reads `−0.51S` and triggers insertion. Repeating the blocks oscillates. Even clean tracking allows a pick roughly half a slot early, costing about **4.17 ms** on the fast grid or **8.34 ms** on the matched grid. |
| Break, missing result or real-only mode | If only unique reals call `assign`, their timestamps advance by `2H` but the pacer advances by `H`. If one missing midpoint never calls `assign`, the next real inherits the missing item’s position. A long pause leaves assignments in the past. If B and its asynchronous midpoint call `assign` in completion order, B can receive the earlier slot. None of these is a slow clock drift. |

**Fix.** Prefer my earlier ceiling rule for this application: reserve both real and midpoint slots **in content order**, using a measured, filtered display grid and the same calibrated earliest-pick rule for the baseline. Apply `N` in whole slots. This preserves deadlines, the matched real-frame differential and recovery from missing content. Raw ceiling on noisy timestamps can itself chatter around a boundary. Filter the grid, constrain reservations to future slots, define collision/drop policy, and re-anchor on cadence, display or delay changes. Count intentional drift, startup alignment, display parity changes and missed content separately. A parity controller driven by actual `presentedTime` must not independently undo a pacer correction. [PLAN.md §6](~/Developer/ps5-framegen/PLAN.md:160) · [Independent plan §5](~/Developer/ps5-framegen/reviews/01-astra-independent-plan.md:74) · [Probe parity feedback](~/Developer/ps5-framegen/probes/present_probe.swift:346)

### Blocker — §5.1 and task 2.1. Implementers have two incompatible cadence specifications

**Evidence.** The plan requires immediate loss of lock on a contradiction or roughly `2H` gap and native mode after eight consecutive new frames. The reference explicitly asserts that lock **survives** either lost copy. It enters native mode with **E 10 of the last 12** verdicts new. The eighth-frame transition is one test’s result after a particular preceding history. The irregular-cadence fixture stays locked until its **E 28th** frame. The saved golden has verdict, state and flags only. It cannot verify `pairValid`, `cancelPending`, the selected endpoints or anything actually shown. [PLAN.md §5.1](~/Developer/ps5-framegen/PLAN.md:107) · [Reference transitions](~/Developer/ps5-framegen/probes/cadence_ref.py:129) · [Drop and menu assertions](~/Developer/ps5-framegen/probes/cadence_ref.py:226) · [Irregular case](~/Developer/ps5-framegen/probes/cadence_ref.py:270) · [Golden schema](~/Developer/ps5-framegen/probes/out/cadence/golden_night_drive_2904_60s.csv:1)

There are two distinct failure cases:

- **Delay can exceed one capture period.** Change only chroma or a HUD pixel between sampled luma positions, then hold that picture. The detector can see zero difference indefinitely. A change below its LOW threshold also has no promotion path unless the *preceding* decision was MID. A reported drop can make even a one-delivered-frame promotion take longer than `H` wall time. With truly identical repeated buffers, a fast pan alone cannot make both copies different. With noisy repeats, a changing overlay or the wrong sampling format, eight high readings can produce false native mode. The recording supplies no live false-positive bound. [Capture skill §§2b, 8](~/Developer/ps5-framegen/skills/capture-cadence/SKILL.md:76) · [Reference promotion](~/Developer/ps5-framegen/probes/cadence_ref.py:80) · [Measured overlapping night distributions](~/Developer/ps5-framegen/reviews/phase1-evidence/cadenceAndQuality.md:23)

In favorable compute phase, `M(A,B)` can be committed to a drawable before C arrives. The cancellation boundary is **selection/submission**, earlier than physical display. Clearing a store cannot retract that submission. A VT completion arriving after the cancellation sweep can also reinsert the invalid item. **Fix.** Separate the detector’s confidence from permission to interpolate a particular timed pair. On the first contradiction, pass current real content, invalidate the pair epoch and leave the real deadline intact. Let mode confidence recover independently. Specify first-sample, static exit, promotion with original first-copy PTS, cut, native exit and missing-copy behavior. Add fixtures that check real IDs, pair IDs, eligibility, commit state and stale completions. Fit on one live trace and score on a separate labeled trace. The offline reference uses the same scalar diff and cannot independently detect a chroma-only miss. [Reference offline check](~/Developer/ps5-framegen/probes/cadence_ref.py:152)
